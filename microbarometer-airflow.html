Six stages between the sky and the sensor.

Follow ambient air as the intake strips out rain, dust and moisture, so the pressure element only sees clean, conditioned air.

Ambient Air

Stage 1

Rain Hood + Protective Mesh

Blocks rain and dust.

Stage 2

Multiple Air Inlets

Silica Gel Pouch

Absorbs moisture and reduces humidity.

Air carries: drier air (moisture absorbed)

Stage 4

SHT45 Sensor

Measures temperature and humidity.

Air carries: drier air, now with temperature and humidity measured

Stage 5

Microbarometer Sensing Chamber

Air reaches the pressure-sensing element.

Air carries: clean, conditioned air and the pressure signal
